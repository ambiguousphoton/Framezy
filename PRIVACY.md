# Privacy Policy for Framezy

**Last updated: 19 September 2026**

Framezy is a shot-tracking app for animators. This policy explains what the app
does and does not do with your information.

The short version: **Framezy collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no
servers.** Everything you type stays on your device.

## What Framezy stores

Framezy saves the following on your device only, so your work is still there when
you reopen the app:

- Projects — name, description, default pipeline, default frame rate, expected
  completion date
- Shots — code, description, frame count, frame rate, colour, pipeline stage,
  version number, due date, and running order
- Dailies notes attached to your shots

This is stored using your device's local app storage. It is not uploaded
anywhere, and it is not shared with us or with anyone else.

## What Framezy does not do

- **No accounts.** There is no sign-up, sign-in, or user profile.
- **No servers.** Framezy has no backend. Your data is never transmitted off
  your device by the app.
- **No analytics or tracking.** No usage statistics, crash reporting,
  advertising identifiers, or behavioural profiling.
- **No advertising.** There are no ads and no ad networks.
- **No personal data collection.** Framezy does not ask for or collect your
  name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, camera,
  microphone, or files.
- **No data selling or sharing.** There is no data to sell or share.

## Permissions

Framezy requests no runtime permissions. It does not access your camera,
microphone, location, contacts, calendar, photo library, or files.

## Third-party components

Framezy is built with React Native and Expo, and uses these open-source
libraries for on-device functionality only:

| Component | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `@react-native-async-storage/async-storage` | Saves your projects and shots locally on the device |
| `@react-native-community/datetimepicker` | Shows the system calendar and clock when you pick a date |
| `react-native-safe-area-context` | Lays the interface out around notches and system bars |
| `expo-splash-screen`, `expo-status-bar` | Launch screen and status bar appearance |

None of these components collect or transmit your data on Framezy's behalf.

## Your data, your control

Because all data is local:

- **To export or back up** your data, use your device's standard app backup.
- **To delete everything**, delete individual projects and shots in the app, or
  uninstall Framezy. Uninstalling removes all Framezy data from your device
  permanently. Deleted data cannot be recovered by us, because we never had it.

## Children's privacy

Framezy is a productivity tool and is not directed at children. Since it
collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children
either.

## Changes to this policy

If Framezy's behaviour ever changes in a way that affects your privacy — for
example if optional cloud sync were added — this policy will be updated before
that change ships, and the date at the top will be revised.
